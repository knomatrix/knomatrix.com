See the maths and assumptions

For each channel, response = A × spend / (K + spend). A is the asymptotic response; K is the spend at half that response. Total response is the sum of channel responses. Every sector uses illustrative parameters, not measured benchmarks. The allocation search uses half-percentage-point transfers, retaining 5–60% per channel. It compares 0.5-percentage-point transfers until no improving transfer remains. It does not model uncertainty, interactions or carryover. Exported figures are calculated from the same functions shown here.

The point is not
the demo number.

It is the question the model makes visible: how does the response change at a different spend level?

In a live engagement, the functions would need evidence from your business, explicit controls, validation, uncertainty and operating constraints. That is the work behind a decision-ready recommendation.
