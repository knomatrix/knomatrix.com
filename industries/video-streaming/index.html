THE COMMERCIAL QUESTION

Which investment creates subscribers who stay?

A tentpole release can drive trials and reactivations at the same time that media spending peaks. We separate the business questions: what drove acquisition, which cohorts retained, and what contribution remained after incentives and servicing costs.

Viewing time is a product signal. It is not automatically a measure of incremental financial value.
