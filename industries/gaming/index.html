THE COMMERCIAL QUESTION

Which acquisition channels create customers worth retaining?

For mobile games, installs and payer conversion are early signals. For licensed sportsbook and iGaming operators, verification, first deposits and net gaming revenue require a different economic model. We define the outcome with finance before comparing channels.

Work for gambling businesses is scoped to licensed operations and eligible adult audiences.
