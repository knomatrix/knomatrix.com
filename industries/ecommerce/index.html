THE COMMERCIAL QUESTION

Which channels bring profitable demand rather than discounted volume?

A promotion can raise revenue while reducing contribution. A stockout can suppress demand that advertising created. We connect campaign history with pricing, product availability, order economics and customer cohorts so these effects are not automatically assigned to media.

Use a consistent treatment of returns, shipping subsidies and marketplace fees.
